# Necro Roll Privacy Policy

_Developer: Yaha Games · Effective date: October 26, 2026 · [한국어](#ko)_

This policy explains what information the Necro Roll game ("the app") collects and how it is used.

## 1. Information we collect

**Game progress.** Your progress is stored only on your device. The app has no accounts or sign-in.

**Anonymous gameplay statistics.** The app sends anonymous events (for example: game started, stage cleared, session length) with a random ID generated on your device, to PostHog. We use this only to improve game balance and fix problems. It does not include your name, email, or contacts.

**Advertising.** The app shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device information such as the advertising ID, IP address, and app interactions to serve and measure ads, as described in [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). Where required, you will be asked for consent, and you can reset or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings.

## 2. What we do not do

We do not sell your information. We do not access your contacts, photos, location, or microphone.

## 3. Third-party services

Google AdMob, PostHog. Each service handles data under its own privacy policy.

## 4. Data retention and deletion

Local progress is removed when you uninstall the app. To delete any analytics data linked to your device, email us at the address below and we will delete it within 30 days.

## 5. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 6. Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version is always on this page.
